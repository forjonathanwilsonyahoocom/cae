**attention-fragmentation.md** — the idea that when multiple agents operate in parallel, each piece of work gets a sliver of attention rather than full focus. In single-agent dev, you can hold the entire design in your 
head; with N agents, the "attention budget" splits across them. This isn't just about context windows — it's about *cognitive load distribution*. A single agent can reason deeply on one problem; a swarm of agents each 
reasons shallowly on many problems. The risk is that no one agent reaches the depth needed for architectural decisions.
